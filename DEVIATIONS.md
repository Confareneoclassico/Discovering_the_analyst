# DEVIATIONS.md

Log of departures from the pre-registered protocol (Zenodo DOI to be recorded
at deposit). Empty at deposit. Each entry records: date; section of the
protocol affected; what changed; why; the analysis state at the time of the
change (which runs existed, whether any claim rule had been evaluated); and
which reported results are affected. Results affected by a deviation are
reported separately from the pre-registered analysis.
