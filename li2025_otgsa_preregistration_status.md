## Compute (benchmarked 5 November)

Timed in the sandbox (one core of an Intel Xeon at 2.1 GHz) with the authors' pinned stack (tsgm 0.0.7, TensorFlow 2.13, scikit-learn 1.3.2) on random arrays of the study's shapes, no real or synthetic data and no claim rule: VAE (tsgm vae_conv5, three categories, 3,000 epochs) 0.49 h, CGAN (cgan_base_c4_l1, 1,500 epochs, batch 100, 0.65 s per step) 2.73 h, RCGAN (cGAN_LSTMn, 1,800 epochs, 0.063 s per step) 0.31 h, so 3.5 core-hours per training at paper settings and about 3.4 averaged over the design. Two threads barely help the CGAN (0.47 s per step), so one single-threaded training per core is the efficient layout. G8's grid-search level costs about 10 minutes per classifier (360 fits plus a refit), three per evaluation, in a quarter of the 4,096 evaluations: about 500 core-hours. Totals: about 2,350 core-hours for the full design (512 trainings plus 20 noise-floor replicates), about 1,220 if the stopping rule ends it at 256. Sam's Mac is Apple Silicon (core count not known): roughly one to two weeks of continuous running for the full design; PG's CI TASK runs the Kraken cluster as an alternative.

## Under discussion (5 November)

Sam asked about treating the benchmark thresholds as uncertain, in the style of a sensitivity analysis of the sensitivity analysis. Proposed: primary verdicts stay at the frozen thresholds (the reason G9 was withdrawn); a secondary, descriptive layer uses that each thresholded rule reduces to one stored statistic per evaluation (X1a: largest of the 18 medians; X1a': of the six C1234 medians; X2: largest absolute correlation difference, ordering as a fixed factor; X3: the 258/30,000 quantile of the evaluation's sum-check errors). With tau independent of the pipeline, S_tau = Var_tau[F_T(tau)]/[pbar(1-pbar)] and pipeline indices use G(T_i) = P(tau >= T_i) in place of the indicator, in closed form. Ranges: those already frozen for the threshold curves. A second layer would vary the reading settings (robust and fragile cut-offs, interval level, M, floors) and report shares of the setting space and second-level Sobol' indices, without decisions to avoid a regress of thresholds. Options: v0.6 blue text before the freeze, or a registered amendment with the code deposit. No extra training needed.
